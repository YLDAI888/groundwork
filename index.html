Groundwork AU

Australian money and living questions, answered with public data.

Groundwork AU is a YouTube channel. Each video takes one concrete question — did wages keep up with prices, what a rate rise does to a household budget — and answers it with official data from the Australian Bureau of Statistics, the Reserve Bank of Australia and other public sources. English narration, with a Mandarin audio track.

Watch on YouTube: @groundworkau

General information only, not financial advice. Narration uses an AI voice; charts are made by Groundwork AU from the cited data.
