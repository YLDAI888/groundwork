Privacy Policy

Effective 9 October 2026

What this covers

This policy covers "GROUNDWORK ops", a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Groundwork AU (@groundworkau) to run that channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses

  • The owner's own YouTube channel, through the YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API and YouTube Reporting API: uploading and scheduling videos, editing titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, reading and replying to comments on the channel's own videos, and reading the channel's analytics.
  • The owner's own Gmail inbox for the channel account, read-only, through the Gmail API: to notice messages from YouTube and Google about the channel (for example policy, copyright or Partner Program notices).

How the data is used and stored

Data is used only to operate and improve the owner's own channel. It is stored on the owner's computer. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models. Public comments on the channel are read only to answer them and to plan future videos.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
